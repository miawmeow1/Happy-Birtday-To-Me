## 🌷 About This Repository

**Happy Birthday To Me** is my personal journal dedicated to myself.

Here, I write letters to the person I am today and the person I hope to become tomorrow. Every entry is a small reminder that my life matters, my feelings are valid, and my journey deserves to be remembered.

This is a place to celebrate not only the days I was born, but also the little moments that make life meaningful.

## 💌 A Letter to Myself

Dear me,

Thank you for making it this far.

I know not every chapter has been easy. There have been moments when I questioned myself, held on to things I couldn't control, and wished certain stories had ended differently.

Some people came into my life and became more meaningful than I expected. Some connections remained undefined, and some dreams did not turn into reality.

But I am learning that not everything I love is meant to stay, and not every unanswered question needs to become a lifelong burden.

I can miss someone without waiting for them. I can cherish a memory without living in the past. I can love deeply without forgetting to love myself.

As I grow older, I hope I become gentler with my heart, braver with my decisions, and wiser about where I invest my time and energy.

I hope I stop measuring my worth by who chooses me and start recognizing the life I can build for myself.

**I deserve a life that feels like mine.**

Happy birthday, and happy every ordinary day in between.

With love,

*Me, always.* 🤍
